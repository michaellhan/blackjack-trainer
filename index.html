About

Basic strategy and Hi-Lo counting, graded as you go

Rules

  • 6 decks, reshuffled after 75% of the shoe
  • Blackjack pays 3:2
  • Dealer on soft 17
  • Dealer peeks for blackjack with an A or 10 up, so you only ever lose your original bet to it
  • Double on any first two cards; double after split
  • Split up to 4 hands; split aces get one card each; 21 after a split isn't blackjack
  • Late surrender

Decks, dealer soft 17, double after split and surrender can be changed in Settings. The chart and grading follow them.

Modes

  • Play: full hands, flat $10 bets from a $1,000 bankroll.
  • Count: a full shoe where you count while you play. Bet off the true count, decide on insurance, and use the index plays. Every bet and play is graded, with random count checks and a summary after each shoe. The true count only shows after a mistake, unless you turn on the count in Settings.
  • Running count drill (under Count): cards flash at your speed, then you enter the count.

Counting

  • Hi-Lo: 2–6 are +1, 7–9 are 0, 10s and aces are −1.
  • True count = running count ÷ decks left. Rounding or flooring are both accepted.
  • Game bets: $10 up to TC +1, then $20 / $40 / $60 / $80 at +2 / +3 / +4 / +5 and up.
  • Game plays use basic strategy plus the Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 index plays (insurance at TC +3, or +2 with 2 decks). They're listed in the chart (C) for the current rules. With 2 decks the index numbers are simulated rather than published ones.
  • Press K in Play or Game to check your count of every card seen this shoe (the hole card counts once it's flipped). The count resets with each new shoe.

Grading

  • Your move is checked against the chart for the current rules.
  • When the chart says double or surrender but you can't (3+ cards, or after a split), the fallback is correct: Ds stands, and surrender falls back to the hit, stand or split shown in the chart's tooltip. Everything else hits.
