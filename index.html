About

Standard basic strategy, graded on every decision

Rules

  • 6 decks, reshuffled after 75% of the shoe
  • Blackjack pays 3:2
  • Dealer on soft 17
  • Dealer peeks for blackjack with an A or 10 up, so you only ever lose your original bet to it
  • Double on any first two cards; double after split
  • Split up to 4 hands; split aces get one card each; 21 after a split isn't blackjack
  • Late surrender

Dealer soft 17, double after split and surrender can be changed in Settings. The chart and grading follow them.

Modes

  • Drill: random spots, skipping no-brainers (naturals, hard 18+). The dealer has already checked for blackjack.
  • Play: full hands, flat $10 bets from a $1,000 bankroll.

Grading

  • Your move is checked against the chart for the current rules.
  • When the chart says double or surrender but you can't (3+ cards, or after a split), the fallback is correct: Ds means stand, otherwise hit.
